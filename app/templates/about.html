Who We Are

Pakistan's Most Trusted
Real Estate Platform

PrimeKey Estates was founded with a single vision — to make buying, selling and renting properties in Pakistan transparent, efficient and stress-free. We connect genuine buyers with verified sellers through a network of professional, licensed agents.

From houses and flats to commercial plots and shops, our comprehensive platform serves every real estate need under one roof. With advanced search, deal tracking, installment plans and digital token receipts, we bring the entire property journey online.
